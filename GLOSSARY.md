# Macro Mint domain language

Macro Mint is a private nutrition journal with individual accounts, meal records and access managed separately from measurement.

## Nutrition

**Food**:
A reusable nutrient profile tied to a named ingredient or packaged product.

**Custom food**:
A food profile saved by one account using nutrition supplied or reviewed by that person.

**Diary entry**:
A dated record of how much of a food was actually eaten, with its nutritional snapshot.

**Portion**:
The amount selected for a diary entry; the eaten portion may be less than the plated amount.

**Favorite**:
A personal shortcut to a reusable food. A favorite is not a diary entry or a meal.

**Saved meal**:
A named reusable group of eaten food portions. Reusing a saved meal creates new diary entries.

**Known nutrient**:
A nutrient quantity with a reported numeric value, including a measured zero.

**Unknown nutrient**:
A nutrient quantity without a trustworthy numeric value. Unknown is not equivalent to zero.

**Complete day**:
A day for which every logged food provides the nutrient being summarized.

## Accounts and access

**Regular account**:
A personal account created without a beta invitation.

**Invited beta tester**:
An account created using a verified single-use beta invitation.

**Trial**:
Temporary access to the application's tracking features.

**Entitlement**:
The server-authoritative permission to use gated app functionality.

**Recovery code**:
A single-use secret that allows an account holder to recover a username-based login.

**Rewarded unlock**:
Account access earned through a reward confirmed by a trusted provider, not merely a client click.
