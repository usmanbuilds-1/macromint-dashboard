# Sign users out when product access ends or a password is recovered

The owner decided on 2026-11-05 that **trial expiry and owner-driven access revocation sign affected users out**, rather than leaving them signed in at a gated screen. Password recovery should similarly terminate other sessions where supported and require fresh authentication. These are distinct events: expiry is time-based, revocation is an owner's decision, and recovery rotates credentials.

Product authorization must be checked server-side on protected operations at request time. Clearing browser storage or redirecting to `/login` is not a substitute for that check; terminating refresh sessions does not guarantee already-issued JWTs instantly become invalid. After a user's sign-out, their account and historical diary are not silently deleted. On 2026-11-05 the owner further approved sign-out **across all devices** as those devices reconnect or refresh, while the backend must immediately reject protected operations regardless of existing JWT lifetime. Already-expired trial users can authenticate into a restricted **account-only** area for data export, account deletion and requests for reactivation, but not for diary changes or AI scanning. This limited identity verification does not restore a trial.

**Status:** accepted behavioral goal; implementation, trusted remote-session invalidation and revocation latency not yet verified. Owner-revoked users' account-only rights are a distinct open decision.
