# Invitations admit testers, not permanently free users

Macro Mint's invitation-only beta controls **who may create an account**, not **how long that account may use paid or trial-gated features**. The owner rejected automatic complimentary access for all beta testers on 2026-11-05. A tester enters the normal configurable trial system (currently configured for 14 days), and only a separately authorized entitlement action can extend or override access. This keeps one consistent entitlement model across private beta and future public enrollment, at the cost of needing an explicit expiry experience while payments are unavailable.

**Status:** accepted; expiry UX and post-trial read/export rights are still to be decided.
