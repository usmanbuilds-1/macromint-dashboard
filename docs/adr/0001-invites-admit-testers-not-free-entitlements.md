# Invitations admit testers, not permanently free users

Macro Mint's invitation-only beta controls **who may create an account**, not **how long that account may use paid or trial-gated features**. The owner rejected automatic complimentary access for all beta testers on 2026-11-05. A tester enters the normal configurable trial system (currently configured for 14 days), and only a separately authorized entitlement action can extend or override access. This keeps one consistent entitlement model across private beta and future public enrollment.

On 2026-11-05 the owner further chose **automatic sign-out on trial expiry**, rather than remaining signed in to a locked app. Sign-out must not be confused with deleting the account or its diary. What expired users can do to reactivate access or export/delete data **after being signed out** remains undecided.

**Status:** accepted; account-only re-entry rights after expiry require clarification and implementation.
